Longitude is a personal record-keeping app for laboratory results. It has no
account, no login, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content and no
network connection. All of it works offline.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached. Recorded on iPhone, latest iOS, from app launch.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Lab results arrive as separate PDFs months or years apart. A single result
tells you little; the trend is what matters. Longitude reads a person's own
reports and draws each measurement as a timeline, so they can see how it has
moved. Audience: adults who have blood tests periodically and want their
own history in one place.
Longitude reports; it does not interpret. It shows the number the lab printed
and the reference interval the lab printed beside it. No diagnosis, no advice,
no risk score, no recommendation.

3. SETTING UP AND ACCESSING FEATURES
No credentials needed. The app opens empty. Quickest path, no file transfer:
  1. Tap "Add a report", then "Paste the text".
  2. Paste SAMPLE A below. Tap "Read results", then "Save".
  3. Repeat with SAMPLE B, an earlier date for the same fictional patient.
  4. Tap any row (try Total cholesterol or HbA1c) for the timeline chart,
     with the lab's reference interval shaded behind it.
  5. The "Reports" tab lists both and can delete either.
"Choose a PDF", "Photograph or pick an image" (on-device text recognition)
and "Enter results by hand" are the other import paths. Any result can also
be corrected by tapping it before saving. Both samples are fictional and
contain no real patient data.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. The app makes no network requests and contains no networking code, no
third-party SDK, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier,
no authentication service, no payment processor and no data provider. It uses
only Apple frameworks, all on device: PDFKit (PDF text), Vision (on-device
text recognition), Swift Charts (timelines), and on iOS 26 with Apple
Intelligence hardware, FoundationModels - Apple's on-device language model -
to help read unusual report layouts. That inference is local; no report text
is transmitted. It is optional: the built-in parser handles everything on
devices without it. No external AI service is used. Reports are stored in the
app's own container and leave the device only in the user's encrypted backup.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Behaviour is identical everywhere. No region-gated features, no
region-specific content, no server to vary by locale. It reads whatever units
and intervals the report contains - conventional (mg/dL) and SI (mmol/L) are
both supported - so it works with reports from any country.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Longitude is not a medical device and needs no licence or authorisation. It
does not diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent disease, calculates no
clinical score, and gives no health advice or warning. It is a record-keeping
and charting tool, equivalent to a user typing their own results into a
spreadsheet and plotting them.
It contains no protected third-party material. Every number shown, including
the reference interval and the in-range indication, is read from the user's
own report as printed by their own laboratory. Longitude publishes no
reference intervals or clinical guidelines of its own. All content is supplied
by the user, is their own health record, and stays on their device.

Your lab results, as a timeline

See how your results have actually changed

Blood tests arrive as PDFs, get read once, and are never seen again. Longitude turns them into a continuous timeline, so you can finally see the trend rather than a single number in isolation.

Everything stays on your iPhone.

Reports are read on the device. No account, no server, no analytics — your health data is never uploaded anywhere.

What it does

  • Reads your reports — add a PDF or photograph and the results are extracted automatically.
  • Joins up the naming — a 2019 report saying "SGPT" and a 2025 one saying "ALT" land on the same line.
  • Reconciles units — mmol/L and mg/dL plotted on one comparable scale.
  • Uses your lab's own reference intervals — never a substituted generic range.
  • Exports — a spreadsheet of everything, for you or your doctor.
Longitude does not interpret your results.

It shows your values and how they compare with the range printed on your report. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Discuss your results with a qualified healthcare professional.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Longitude collects no data, and your health information never leaves your iPhone.

There is no account, no server and no analytics. Your lab reports are read on the device itself. We cannot see your results, because they are never sent to us.

Information we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive nothing from the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following into its own private storage on your iPhone:

  • Test results read from your reports — analyte names, values, units and reference intervals
  • Report dates and lab names
  • The original PDF or photograph of each report, if you add one

This is held in the app's private container. It is included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone, and is deleted permanently when you delete the app.

How your reports are read

Text is extracted from PDFs and photographs entirely on your device, using Apple's built-in PDF and text-recognition frameworks. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, an on-device language model may assist with unusual report layouts. That model runs locally on your iPhone. No part of your report is transmitted anywhere, and nothing is sent to Apple, to us, or to any third party.

Camera and photo library

If you photograph a report or choose an image, iOS asks your permission. The image is copied into the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, and the app cannot see any other photo in your library.

Third parties

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is shared with anyone.

Not medical advice

Longitude records and displays your results and shows how they compare with the reference interval printed on your own report. It does not interpret results, diagnose conditions, or offer medical advice, and it is not a medical device. Always discuss your results with a qualified healthcare professional.

Children

Longitude is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You control your records entirely: delete a report to remove its results, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the app were ever to collect data, that would be described here and you would be asked to consent first.
